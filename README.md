# Wavecon Telecom: 5G Rollout Business Impact Analysis

An end-to-end Power BI business intelligence project evaluating the post-launch performance of Wavecon Telecom's 5G deployment. The analysis investigates key shifts in revenue, customer churn, active user engagement, and plan-level performance before and after the 5G rollout to deliver strategic recommendations for leadership.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

Wavecon Telecom launched its 5G network across key markets. This analysis evaluates the commercial and operational impact of that launch against baseline pre-5G metrics. 

While the new 5G plan showed strong initial uptake, the transition period exposed significant retention risks and overall revenue contraction that require targeted pricing and operational intervention.

### Key Metrics at a Glance
* **Total Revenue:** Decreased by **0.50%** post-launch.
* **Active User Base:** Net decline of approximately **7 million users**.
* **Churn:** Unsubscribed users increased by **~1.4 million**.
* **Top Legacy Plan (P1):** Remained the highest grossing plan at **₹4,199M**.
* **Most Impacted Plan (P7):** Experienced a **73.28%** decline in revenue post-rollout.
* **New 5G Offering (P13):** Generated **₹1.9B** in post-launch revenue, leading new product adoption.

---

## 🔍 Business Questions Addressed

1. **Macro Financial Impact:** Did the 5G rollout generate positive top-line growth, or did customer attrition outpace premium pricing gains?
2. **KPI Disruption:** Which core operating KPIs (Active Users, Unsubscribes, ARPU) underperformed post-launch?
3. **Plan Cannibalization & Performance:** Which legacy plans retained stability, which suffered cannibalization, and which 5G plans drove adoption?
4. **Plan Discontinuation:** What performance patterns justified sunsetting specific underperforming tiers?

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

* **Address Mid-Tier Churn:** Investigate whether technical coverage gaps or uncompetitive pricing caused the steep decline in legacy plans like P7.
* **Accelerate Migration to P13:** Leverage P13's ₹1.9B momentum by creating targeted upgrade incentives for remaining legacy subscribers.
* **Retention Interventions:** Establish an automated retention trigger for customer cohorts showing drop-off patterns following the 5G switch.
* **Network & Coverage Transparency:** Clarify 5G coverage boundaries to eliminate user dissatisfaction stemming from premature device onboarding in spotty service zones.
